# Lowered to 289 on WOR-2065, 2026-08-02, working the triage tail. Ten of
# the twenty-three are a measurement fix and thirteen are real narrowings.
#
# The measurement fix is in `cfg_test_line_ranges`. The scan finds the
# extent of a `#[cfg(test)]` block by counting braces forward from the
# attribute, and `#[cfg(test)] mod test_env;` has no braces at all, so the
# count ran on until the next `{` anywhere below and swallowed whatever
# opened it. In `sbproxy-config/src/lib.rs` that was the
# `pub use config_authority::{...}` façade ten lines further down, so three
# publicly re-exported constants were filed as test-only. Ten candidates
# were wrong this way across five crates, and every one of them would have
# been a hard E0365 had anyone taken the scan's advice and narrowed it. The
# scan now stops at the first `;` it reaches before any brace.
#
# The thirteen narrowings are the whole of what the tests-only slice had
# left that could be narrowed at all. Six had a production caller inside
# their own file and became `pub(crate)`: ADRF_VERSION, start_with_cipher,
# mint_proof, as_legacy_str, resolved_headers, put_meta. Seven are helpers
# whose own rustdoc says they exist for tests, and they became
# `#[cfg(test)] pub(crate)` rather than plain `pub(crate)`, because a bare
# narrowing of an item with no production caller is the dead_code failure
# WOR-2065 already hit once on with_reverse_error: shadow_dropped_value,
# shadow_timeout_value, ratelimit_rejected_value, clear_resolved_base,
# set_applied_authority, clear_applied_bundle,
# reset_sink_dispatcher_for_test.
#
# What is left in the 289 is not a backlog of narrowings. 279 of them are
# reached by an integration test under `crates/<name>/tests/`, which
# compiles as its own crate, so `pub` is the only visibility that resolves
# and no cleanup can lower the number. The remaining 10 carry an
# `escalated` verdict in scripts/pub-item-verdicts.json, each with a
# question for whoever owns the subsystem, because the answer changes
# behaviour rather than visibility. Do not expect this number to fall much
# further without the settlement crate's origin gate work landing.
